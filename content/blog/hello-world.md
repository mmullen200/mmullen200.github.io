# Hello World

This is the first post on my new site. I'm Michael Mullen -- a game producer and AI strategist -- and I've been wanting a place to write about the things that occupy my brain.

## Why this site exists

I spend a lot of time thinking about **technology, AI, games, and systems**. Notes pile up in Obsidian. Ideas connect to other ideas. But most of it stays private, locked in a vault that only I can see.

This site changes that. It's built with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/), which turns my Obsidian vault into a published digital garden. That means this isn't a traditional blog with a strict publishing schedule -- it's a living collection of notes and writing that grows and evolves over time.

## What to expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Game production** -- lessons learned from shipping products and managing teams
- **AI strategy** -- how organizations can actually use AI, beyond the hype
- **Tech** -- tools, code, and workflows that make life better
- **Systems thinking** -- patterns that show up everywhere once you start looking

Some posts will be polished. Others will be rough working notes. That's the point of a digital garden -- ideas at every stage of development.

## Let's go

If any of that sounds interesting, stick around. More to come.
